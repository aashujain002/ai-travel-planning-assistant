## Why these sources?

1. **Why is Wikivoyage useful for our RAG knowledge base?**  
   Wikivoyage provides broad, traveller-focused coverage in a structured format, including neighbourhoods, attractions, transport, food, practical tips, and cultural context. This breadth helps the RAG system answer exploratory questions and recommend options across many travel needs. Because it is community maintained, its content should be treated as useful general guidance and supplemented with authoritative sources for time-sensitive facts.

2. **Why do we want an official Visit Singapore source?**  
   An official tourism source offers authoritative destination information, such as visitor guidance, featured attractions, seasonal campaigns, and locally endorsed experiences. It improves the reliability of recommendations and provides a trusted reference when the assistant needs to distinguish official guidance from community-generated advice. Information that can change frequently, such as opening hours, entry requirements, prices, and event availability, should still be verified at response time.

3. **Why are itinerary examples useful for a travel-planning assistant?**  
   Itinerary examples show how attractions can be grouped by location, theme, duration, and traveller type. They give the assistant practical patterns for sequencing activities, balancing pace, and adapting plans to available time. The assistant can use these patterns as inspiration while tailoring each itinerary to the user's interests, budget, dates, mobility needs, and real-time constraints.

> **Assignment scope:** The assistant may use accommodation information as general travel context, but it does not provide hotel or flight booking, reservations, navigation, or payment processing.
