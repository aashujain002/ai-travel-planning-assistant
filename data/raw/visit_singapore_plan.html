      What You Need To Know About Singapore

      Find useful transport details and practical essentials to help you navigate Singapore smoothly and confidently.

      Travel Tips

      Visa & Entry Requirements

      Ensure smooth entry to Singapore by knowing visa requirements—visa-free short visits for most, while extended stays may need specific visas like work or student visas.

      Find out more requirements

      Safe Drinking Water

      Water in Singapore passes World Health Organization standards. You can drink water straight from the tap.

      Smoking Area

      Smoking is not allowed in air-conditioned places such as malls and eateries. Designated smoking areas, which are demarcated with notices, overhead signs and/or markings on the ground.

      Power Plug

      The standard electrical current in Singapore is 220-240 volts AC (50 cycles). Power plugs have three square prongs.

      Language

      You can converse in English with Singaporeans. Additionally, many locals speak other languages, often including Mandarin, Malay, Tamil, and Singlish.

      Learn how to speak Singlish

      Weather & Timezone

      Singapore features a tropical climate (25-33ºC) all year-round, characterised by abundant rainfall and high humidity. It is situated in the GMT +8 time zone.

      Money and Payment

      Payment Methods

      Singapore dollars or credit cards are the preferred method to pay for goods and services. Contactless payment via Apple Pay and Google Pay are also available at most shops and restaurants.

      Tipping

      While tipping is not customary, should you experience good service, you are encouraged to do so.

      Tax Refund

      Tourists can claim a refund on the 8% Goods and Services Tax (GST) paid on your purchases if you spend more than $100 at any participating shops.

      Find out more

      Commuting around the island

      Public Transport

      MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) trains and buses offer an efficient and reliable network across SIngapore. Smaller LRT (Light Rapid Transit) trains are also available in some areas.

      Ways to Pay

      There are many ways to pay for your public transport rides. You can use contactless bank cards, mobile wallets, concession cards, EZ-Link cards, cash, or a Singapore Tourist Pass - which offers all-day unlimited travel.

      Find out more

      Distance Fares

      You will get a better deal and greater convenience when you use any of the payment methods except cash for your travel, as travel fares are charged based on the total distance travelled across different travel modes.

      USE A FARE CALCULATOR

      Accessibility Options

      All public buses are wheelchair accessible and open strollers are allowed. Please approach bus captains or train staff if you need assistance.

      Mass Rapid Transit Map

      For convenient navigation throughout the city, you can download the Singapore MRT map, which provides a comprehensive overview of the efficient metro system.

      Get a MRT map

      Taxi or Private Hire Car

      Hail a taxi by the roadside at most places, or by queuing for one at a taxi stand, which you’ll find at most malls, hotels and attractions.

      Taxi Fares

      Cabs in Singapore are metered, and surcharges vary with timing, route and choice of service used. To get a rough idea of the final fare, check with the driver on the surcharges and ask for a receipt at the end of the trip.

      Ride Hailing Apps

      Ride hailing applications available in Singapore let you book a ride from your preferred pick-up point to your desired destination. Options available include ComfortDelGro, Grab, Gojek, Ryde and TADA.
